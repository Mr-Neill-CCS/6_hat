How are these scores calculated?

Each hat is the average of five questions. To match the original spreadsheet, the page also compares each hat average with your overall response average and standard deviation, then converts that z-score to a normal-distribution percentile.

Important: this percentile is relative to your own pattern of answers. It does not mean you scored higher than that percentage of other people.
